# Nine-hole routing and bunker strategy — November 3, 2026

Original Fairway & Folly designs; no copied hole layouts, artwork or course assets. References consulted: [Pebble Beach official course](https://www.pebblebeach.com/golf/pebble-beach-golf-links/), [Pinehurst No. 2](https://www.pinehurst.com/golf/courses/no-2/), [official Masters course map](https://www.masters.com/pdf/2026%20Masters%20Course%20Map.pdf). The design draws on coastal approach risk, Pinehurst's crowned-green/sand strategy, and flowering creek corridors respectively. It does not reproduce any named championship hole.

| Hole | Strategy and bunker placement |
|---|---|
| 1 | Move the left drive bunker to the normal full-driver landing depth, enlarge it, and place the other two immediately beside the approach/green. A straight centered drive remains available. |
| 2 | Put sand on the playable side of each safe shelf and the risky center island; guard the green shoulder. The shelves still support the tested safe three-shot and risky two-shot routes. |
| 3 | Retain compact greenside sand on the limited island collar. A larger bowl here would overlap the green or water. The pots remain escapable with a lofted wedge. |
| 4 | Pinch the dogleg landing shelf from both sides and guard the raised green's front, left and right. A well-powered second shot must carry the front trap. |
| 5 | Drive trap now bites farther into the landing width; two layup-depth traps tighten the approach; both greenside hazards sit beside the green rather than remote rough. |
| 6 | Bring the back and front-left bunkers to approach misses and the right pot to the green shoulder. |
| 7 — Seabell Headland | 202-yard par 3; a long hybrid across an inlet, broad right shoulder, three greenside traps, shoreline spillway and stone bell landmark. Seabell Court is a sheared lens with diagonal bank fins and a side pool. |
| 8 — Copperpine Bend | 460-yard par 4, dogleg left; drive to the right shelf then turn left toward a crowned green. Inside-corner and right-side drive sand force placement; three green guards. Warm sandhill turf, stone pines, and Pinecone Terrace's bank/planter caroms. |
| 9 — Azalea Crown | 602-yard par 5; a three-shot flowering-creek finale. Paired drive traps, a layup trap, two green guards, raised final green and waterfall. Petal Conservatory has a moving gate plus open outside bank routes. |

35 groomed bunkers total, 22 on holes 1–6 and 13 on the new holes. Rendering, sand lies and depressed floors use the same outlines. All sampled collars clear green, water, tee and bounds. Reachable shot dispersions include sand finishes on every original hole. This is a strategic sampling check rather than a measured real-player hit probability.

Every club loses distance from sand. Flat carry at full power is approximately Driver 74 yd, Hybrid 77 yd, 7 iron 76 yd, Wedge 61 yd, Sand wedge 43 yd. The lower-lofted driver is deliberately inefficient from sand. The UI shows these reduced estimates on the club buttons and shot meter; terrain and wind still affect actual results. All deep pots retain tested sand-wedge exits.

The straight accuracy window is 6.5% of bar width for Driver, 9% Hybrid, 12% 7 iron, 16% Wedge, and 21% Sand wedge. Green-zone hits produce zero hook/slice input; misses ramp continuously toward the existing severe extreme miss. Display, cursor feedback and physics use the same club data. Power timing remains unchanged.

Solo rounds and private multiplayer support 1–9 holes. Nine-hole total: par 36, 3,431 yd. Existing shorter round scorecards and invitations remain supported. The additive Supabase migration updates length constraints and match creation validation without modifying old matches, scores, invitations or access rules.
